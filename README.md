<h1 align="center">Horizontal Velocity Speedometer for Any TF2 HUD</h1>

<h2>Instructions</h2>
<ol>
  <li>Drag and drop the <code>materials</code> and <code>resource</code> folders into your HUD's directory.</li>
  <li>Go into the <code>_choose_your_option_</code> folder and open the folder for your desired option.</li>
  <li>Drag and drop the <code>materials</code> folder from your chosen option into your HUD's directory.</li>
  <li>Open your HUD's <code>...\resource\ui\hudplayerclass.res</code> file with Notepad and add <code>#base "speedometer.res"</code> at the very top of the file.</li>
</ol>
